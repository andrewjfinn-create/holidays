The idea

A culture weekend, done easily

An hour and a quarter from Heathrow, Amsterdam is ideal for a bank-holiday escape. We stay right on the Amstel, in the old centre, so the canals, Dam Square and the Nine Streets are on the doorstep and the great museums are a short walk or tram ride away. Everything is timed to miss the queues: a private guide at the Rijksmuseum, first slots at Anne Frank House, a chauffeured run to Keukenhof and our own boat on the canals.

Where we're staying

Tivoli Doelen

Old centre · Three nights

One of Amsterdam's oldest hotels, right on the water

The Doelen has stood on the Amstel since the 1880s, on the site of the old civic guard's headquarters where Rembrandt's Night Watch first hung. Restored by Tivoli, it keeps the domed corner tower, the canal-side terrace and rooms looking over the river.

From here you can walk to Dam Square, the Royal Palace, the flower market and the Nine Streets. The museum quarter is 15–20 minutes away on foot, or a few stops on tram 2 or 12.

Good to know

The practical bits

Weather

Early May typically brings highs of around 18° and lows near 8°, long light evenings, and a chance of showers. Pack layers and a light raincoat.

Money

Euros. Contactless cards and Apple Pay work almost everywhere, including trams; Amsterdam is close to cashless. No special payment apps needed.

Taxis

Uber and Bolt both work well. For a classic cab, TCA (Taxi Centrale Amsterdam); every licensed taxi must take cards.

Say it in Dutch

Please: alstublieft (AHL-stuu-bleeft) · Thank you: dank u wel (DAHNK uu vel) · Hello: hallo. Almost everyone speaks excellent English.

Holidays

King's Day is the Tuesday before (27 April). We fly home on the UK bank holiday, Monday 3 May. Remembrance Day (4 May) and Liberation Day (5 May) follow just after.

What's on

The Music On festival takes over part of the city on 1–2 May, and it's the Dutch May school holiday, so every museum ticket is pre-booked.

Passports

Valid for at least three months beyond our return. On British passports the new EU entry checks apply; we'll check ETIAS before we go.

Plugs & water

European two-pin plugs, so bring adaptors. Tap water is excellent. Watch out for the red bike lanes.
